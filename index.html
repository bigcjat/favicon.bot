Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best format for a favicon in 2026?

In 2026, the best practice is to use an SVG favicon for modern browsers, accompanied by a high-resolution PNG (like 192x192 or 512x512) for Apple Touch Icons and Android Home Screens, and a 32x32 ICO file as a fallback for legacy browsers.

Does this favicon generator upload my images to a server?

No. Favicon.bot uses WebAssembly (WASM) to run the FFmpeg conversion engine entirely inside your local web browser. Your images are never uploaded to any server, ensuring 100% privacy and instant conversion speeds.

How do I add a favicon to my HTML website?

Extract your downloaded favicon zip file into the root or public directory of your website. Then, paste the generated HTML link tags into the <head> section of your index.html file.
